74% of the brands surveyed increased their investment in affiliate and partnership marketing in 2024. Not as a trend, but out of conviction: partnerships deliver measurable results with a calculable risk. You mainly pay when something actually happens.

What the Latest Report Shows

The State of Affiliate Marketing Report by impact.com makes it clear: 71% of the marketers surveyed find affiliate marketing more cost-effective than other channels. 66% report a stronger return on ad spend. The direction is clear: away from wasted reach, toward partnerships with results.

The Five Pillars of Modern Partnerships

1. Diversify Your Partner Mix

Successful programs use 3 to 4 different partner types, from influencers and content creators to loyalty programs. If you rely on a single channel, you only reach clients at one point in their decision.

2. Steer Investment by Results

38% of top-performing brands invest 21 to 30% of their marketing budget in partnerships. The key difference: they don't see partnerships as a cost center, but as a profit driver. Which metrics matter is explained in our article on performance marketing.

3. Use AI Strategically

97% of brands already use AI in their affiliate strategy, but most only for 2 to 3 use cases. The opportunity lies in automation: finding the right partners, forecasting results, improving content.

4. Creator Partnerships at Eye Level

Social media creators show the strongest growth of all partner types (up 14 percentage points). But the key isn't volume, it's long-term, credible relationships.

5. Rethink Measurement

The best programs don't just measure the last click before a purchase, but each partner's full contribution to the client's decision, from first awareness to repeat purchase.

Partnership marketing isn't a trend. It's the logical evolution of marketing in a world where trust is worth more than reach.

What This Means for Female Entrepreneurs

For female businesses, partnership marketing is especially attractive: you mainly pay for results, use the trust others have already built with your audience, and build a network that grows independently of your personal time. That's not marketing, that's infrastructure.
